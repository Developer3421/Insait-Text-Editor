# 🚀 Insait Text Editor - Now Available!

**Insait Text Editor** is a modern, privacy-focused text editor for Windows with a built-in **offline AI assistant**.

Unlike other AI tools, Insait runs 100% locally on your device. No cloud, no subscriptions, no data tracking.

### ✨ Key Features:
*   **📝 Advanced Editor:** Multi-tab support, rich formatting, and "ruled paper" backgrounds.
*   **🤖 Local AI:** Powered by Gemma 3 1B. Chat, brainstorm, and edit text without an internet connection.
*   **🔒 Private:** Your documents and chats never leave your computer.
*   **⚡ Fast & Native:** Built with .NET 10 and Avalonia UI for high performance.
